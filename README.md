# xO Football

Watch the game like a coach. Chart personnel, plays and results live, grade your Keys to the Game, and build your team's book all season.

Open the site on your phone, then Share > Add to Home Screen to launch it full screen.
Your book is saved on the device you chart on. After each game, open Film Room and tap "Back up my book."

Season stats refresh every morning from CollegeFootballData.com.
